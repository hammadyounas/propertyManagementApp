WHEREAS the RECIPIENT is considering entering into a transaction (hereinafter the “Potential Transaction”) with the VENDOR regarding the potential sale of an immovable property situated at [Insert Address of Property] (hereinafter the “Property”).

 

WHEREAS to assist in evaluating the Potential Transaction, the RECIPIENT will obtain confidential information from the VENDOR concerning the VENDOR. The RECIPIENT recognizes that the measures of protection described in the present agreement are for the benefit and protection of the VENDOR, its affiliates and representatives solely.

 

WHEREAS the RECIPIENT thus irrevocably binds itself towards the VENDOR to respect the terms and conditions herein described:

 

1.    The term “Confidential Information” includes specifically but is not limited to:

(a)  Any exchange of information communicated verbally, in writing, or in any other manner whatsoever by the VENDOR or its representatives to the RECIPIENT before and further to the signing of the present document and that are characterized as confidential information, or are of a confidential nature;

 

 

 

(b)  Any information that is communicated verbally, in writing or in any other manner whatsoever by the VENDOR or its representatives to the RECIPIENT regarding the Property, its coordinates, its owner, and any other aspect that concerns the Potential Transaction;

(c)  In the event where the RECIPIENT visits the Property, any information that is obtained by the RECIPIENT during such visit, including but not limited to its location, any leasing and/or sale information and any information relating to the owner;

(d)  Any information that is communicated verbally, in writing or in any other manner whatsoever by the VENDOR or its representatives to the RECIPIENT regarding the VENDOR’s activities, business plans, methods, formulas, ideas, projects and any other information that would be disclosed by the VENDOR or its representatives;

(e)  Any discussions and/or negotiations that concern the Potential Transaction and all the terms, conditions or other related facts to the Potential Transaction, notably their evolution;

 

2.   The RECIPIENT recognizes that the Confidential Information is the property of the VENDOR and that it is private and essential for the VENDOR’s interests and activities. The RECIPIENT also recognizes that disclosure of said information will greatly prejudice the interests and activities of the VENDOR.

3.    The RECIPIENT recognizes that the Confidential Information is transmitted for the sole purpose of facilitating the Potential Transaction. The RECIPIENT undertakes not to use the Confidential Information in any other manner except to assess the Potential Transaction, and, if need be, in order to execute its obligations in virtue of the Potential Transaction.

4.    All right, title and interest in and to the Confidential Information of the VENDOR shall remain the exclusive property of the VENDOR and the Confidential Information of the VENDOR shall be held in trust by the RECIPIENT for the benefit of the VENDOR.

5.    The RECIPIENT shall: (i) use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of determining the feasibility of the Potential Transaction, and, if applicable, concluding the Potential Transaction upon the terms agreed to by the Parties and for no other purpose; (ii) at all times, maintain the confidential nature of the Confidential Information in its possession by taking reasonable steps to protect such Confidential Information from unauthorized use, access and disclosure, such steps to be at least equal to those taken by the RECIPIENT to protect its own Confidential Information and not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without consent of the VENDOR except otherwise permitted herein;

 

 

 

(iii) disclose the Confidential Information solely to the representatives of the RECIPIENT who have a legitimate need to access the Confidential Information and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those contained herein; (iv) refrain from copying or reproducing the Confidential Information, except as required for the purpose of evaluating the Potential Transaction; (v) refrain from reverse engineering, decompiling, disassembling or attempting to learn the source code of any Confidential Information; and (iv) immediately notify the VENDOR upon any actual or threatened breach of this agreement.

6.   If at any time the RECIPIENT becomes aware of any unauthorized duplication, access, use, possession or knowledge of any Confidential Information of the VENDOR, it shall immediately notify the VENDOR in writing. The RECIPIENT shall provide any and all reasonable assistance to the VENDOR to protect the VENDOR's proprietary rights in any Confidential Information that the RECIPIENT or its representatives may have directly or indirectly disclosed or disseminated in breach of the present agreement. The obligations set forth in this paragraph are not exclusive of any of the remedies available under the present agreement or otherwise at law or in equity and all such remedies shall be deemed to be cumulative.

7.    The RECIPIENT will be bound by the present agreement during the entire negotiation and assessment process concerning the Potential Transaction and, subject to paragraph 10 hereinbelow, forever following the signature of the present agreement and acceptation by the RECIPIENT. The RECIPIENT shall no longer be bound by this agreement upon consummation of the Potential Transaction save and except for as concerns Confidential Information received regarding the VENDOR.

8.   The RECIPIENT undertakes in virtue of the present agreement to indemnify the VENDOR for any loss or damage (notably all extrajudicial legal fees) that are a direct or indirect result of the disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the RECIPIENT. The RECIPIENT shall also indemnify and/or hold harmless the VENDOR and its affiliates, related entities, directors, officers, employees and representatives from any reclamation, lawsuit, judicial or administrative action taken against the VENDOR and/or its representatives and any damages that the VENDOR and/or its representatives may suffer as a result or by reason of the failure of the RECIPIENT to comply with the terms and conditions of the present agreement.

9.   The RECIPIENT recognizes that any default by the RECIPIENT to respect any term and condition set forth in the present agreement could cause serious and irreparable harm to the VENDOR and possibly its affiliates, and the RECIPIENT further recognizes that, in the event of such breach, the VENDOR and/or its affiliates may immediately institute the necessary proceedings in order to obtain from the competent judicial authorities an order of provisional interlocutory injunction, order of interlocutory injunction or an order of permanent injunction, without prejudice to any of the rights and recourses of the VENDOR to claim damages and interest and to exercise any and all other recourse available to the VENDOR at law.

10.   If the Potential Transaction should not be concluded for any reason whatsoever, the RECIPIENT undertakes to immediately destroy or remit to the VENDOR any Confidential Information that the RECIPIENT has in its possession. The RECIPIENT also undertakes not to transmit any Confidential Information to anyone whatsoever or to make use of same for their own personal benefit or for the benefit of any third party, related or otherwise.

11.  The RECIPIENT shall not assign or transfer the present agreement, or any right contained in the present agreement to any third party, whether in totality or in part.

12. All obligations of the RECIPIENT in virtue of the present agreement shall be joint and several, should the RECIPIENT be constituted of more than one (1) person or entity.

13.  No waiver of any provisions of the present agreement shall be deemed to constitute a waiver of any other provisions (whether or not similar); nor shall such waiver be binding unless executed in writing by the Party to be bound by the waiver. No failure on the part of a Party to exercise, and no delay in exercising any right under the present agreement shall operate as a waiver of such right; nor shall any single or partial exercise of any such right preclude any other or further exercise of such right or the exercise of any other right.

14.  The present agreement may only be amended, supplemented, or otherwise modified by written agreement signed by the Parties. The present agreement shall be binding upon and enure to the benefit of the Parties and their respective heirs, executors, legal personal representatives, successors and permitted assigns.
