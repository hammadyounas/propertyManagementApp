9.      Arbitration

The Organisme d’autoréglementation du courtage immobilier du Québec (OACIQ) acts as conciliator or mediator in disputes between a broker or an agency and a client, if the parties so request. The same holds for disputes between brokers, between agencies, or between brokers and agencies. If all the parties are members of a real estate board, the OACIQ may only take on this role to protect the public. The OACIQ may also arbitrate accounts between a broker or an agency and a client.

10.    Quebec Real Estate Brokerage Self-Regulatory Organization

The Agency declares being duly registered with the Organisme d’autoréglementation du courtage immobilier du Québec (OACIQ). The mission of the OACIQ is to protect the public. In particular, it ensures that brokerage transactions are carried out in compliance with the Real Estate Brokerage Act. It oversees the activities of real estate brokers and agencies and enforces the rules of professional conduct. The OACIQ issues licenses to real estate brokers and agencies. Consumers may contact the OACIQ to submit a request for assistance or investigation regarding a real estate broker or agency, or to get information on real estate transactions and the oversight of license holders and agencies.
