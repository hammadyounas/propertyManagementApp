                                                            (hereinafter referred to as the “Broker)

                                                            (BUZZ and the Broker referred to as the "Party"

                                                             or the "Parties")

                           

 

WHEREAS the Broker holds a valid real estate broker’s licence (hereinafter referred to as the “Broker’s License”) delivered by the Organisme d’autoréglementation du courtage immobilier du Québec (hereinafter referred to as the “OACIQ”);

 

WHEREAS BUZZ holds a valid licence (hereinafter referred to as the “License”) and is validly registered as an agency with the OACIQ;

 

WHEREAS BUZZ and the Broker wish to enter into an agreement pursuant to which the Broker will provide residential and commercial real estate broker services to BUZZ as set out below, and BUZZ shall retain the services of the Broker as an independent contractor within the meaning of the law;

 

NOW THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS :

1.    PREAMBLE

1.1  The Preamble shall form an integral part of this Agreement;

 

2.     Representations and Warranties

2.1   Both Parties represent that they are fully authorized to enter into this Agreement. The performance and obligations of either Party will not violate or infringe upon the rights of any third-party and any organization, or upon any professional order or governmental regulation;

 

 

 

2.2   The Broker has no authority to bind or obligate BUZZ and shall not obligate or make BUZZ liable in any circumstance except in the ordinary course of business, with the written consent of BUZZ’s director. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee relationship, a partnership, or joint venture between the parties;

2.3   Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Broker does not require BUZZ’s consent for listings, promises to purchase and brokerage mandates;

2.4   The Broker hereby represents and warrants that it is not bound by any non-competition or non-solicitation agreements with any other brokerage agency;

2.5   For the duration of this Agreement, the Broker undertakes not to provide real estate brokerage services on its own account or on behalf of another broker, team or entity other than BUZZ;

 

3.     Compensation

3.1   The Broker shall be compensated in accordance with the compensation agreement attached as Schedule A hereinbelow;

3.2    Any compensation due to the Broker shall be paid to the Broker immediately as the funds are received by BUZZ or as soon thereafter as practicable;

3.3    The Broker undertakes not to share its remuneration, directly or indirectly, with a third party who is not authorized to collect it under the Act respecting the distribution of financial products and services (hereinafter referred to as the "ADFPS");

3.4    The Broker shall immediately notify BUZZ if any third-party Broker is involved and/or to be remunerated in the context of a transaction pursuant to which the Broker renders services according to this Agreement

3.5    The Broker shall not be entitled to any advance or payment from BUZZ against future compensation;

4.    Business Expenses

4.1  Except as specifically provided herein, the Broker shall be solely responsible for the payment of all expenses incurred in the course of his activities, including but not limited to office space, travelling expenses, photocopies, telephone, insurance, licenses, contribution and fees, professional or otherwise and fines of any nature whatsoever. The Broker shall provide its own vehicle and be solely responsible for all expenses related thereto;

4.2   The Broker shall also be solely responsible for all costs related to any professional training and/or corporate events;

 

 

4.3   The Broker shall at all times hold valid Q.S.T. and G.S.T. registration numbers, and all amounts owed to said tax authorities shall be entirely paid by the Broker as they become due. The Broker further acknowledges and hereby consents that BUZZ may cause any amounts owed by the Broker as Q.S.T. and G.S.T. to be paid to said tax authorities from the Broker’s commissions, the whole according to the law;

 

5.     Indemnification

5.1   The Broker agrees to indemnify and hold BUZZ harmless against any and all liability, extrajudicial legal fees, claims, actions, proceedings, complaints or demands, judicial or otherwise, made by or instituted by any third parties, arising from the brokerage activities of the Broker and his business relationship with BUZZ or caused directly or indirectly by any act, negligence or omission of the Broker in the performance of its obligations;

 

6.    Term

6.1  This Agreement shall be in force for a period of one (1) year from the date of its signature (hereinafter referred to as the "Term");

6.2  At the end of the Term, this Agreement will automatically renew on every anniversary of the Agreement unless otherwise terminated as provided for herein below;

7.    Termination

7.1  This Agreement may be terminated at any time without penalty by either party following a written notice of thirty (30) days prior to the date of termination or non-renewal;

7.2  The termination of this Agreement does not release either party from its obligations arising from existing contracts in effect pursuant to this Agreement or from the present Agreement.

7.3   In the event of termination, the parties undertake to cooperate with each other in a professional manner until all legal and contractual obligations incumbent upon the Parties have been duly fulfilled;

7.4   In the case of a default by either party under the terms of this Agreement, the other party shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement if said default is not cured within five (5) days prior written notice to the other party requiring it to remedy to said default;

7.5   Given BUZZ’s obligations pursuant to the Real Estate Brokerage Act, both parties shall inform affected clients of the Broker’s departure from Buzz in a timely manner. It is understood that the parties shall not in any way attempt to influence such clients’ decision and shall respect the choice of the clients;

 

 

 

7.6  Upon termination of this Agreement, the Broker shall cease all use of or reference to BUZZ in any material, sign or promotional environment, including in any electronic address, domain name or other display of any nature whatsoever, including, without limitation, business cards, stationery and any other promotional items or items used in communications or presentations to current or potential clients;

 

8.    Insurance

8.1  The Broker declares being covered by liability insurance in the amount of […] and hereby represents that it has provided a copy of said policy to BUZZ in accordance with regulatory requirements;

8.2  The Broker undertakes to maintain its insurance coverage in force for said amount for the duration of the present Agreement;

8.3  The Broker shall be solely responsible for paying all costs associated with its insurance coverage;

8.4  The Broker shall immediately inform BUZZ of any changes to its insurance coverage, including but not limited to any cancellation of the policy;

 

9.     Broker’s Obligations

9.1   The Broker shall at all times act in accordance with its applicable legislation, rules, by-laws and regulations, BUZZ’s internal policies and rules of professional conduct, its contractual obligations towards third-parties and this Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Rules of Professional Conduct");

9.2   The Broker shall at all times, maintain its independence and avoid placing itself, directly or indirectly, in a situation of conflict of interest;

9.3   The Broker shall immediately report any breach of the Rules of Professional Conduct to BUZZ’s responsible officer (hereinafter referred to as the “AEO”);

9.4   The Broker shall always act professionally with honesty, loyalty and competence;

9.5   The Broker shall act in accordance with its professional knowledge. The Broker its shall obtain the assistance of the AEO as required before making any representations to third-parties which could in his professional opinion bind BUZZ;

 

9.6   The Broker shall at all times ensure the validity of its certificate and/or registration with the OACIQ, during the entirety of this Agreement, and shall provide a copy to the AEO. The Broker shall immediately disclose, in writing, any changes to its status with the OACIQ;

9.7   The Broker shall at all times comply with any requests from the AEO, any staff assigned to BUZZ’s compliance activities or any individual mandated by the OACIQ;

 

 

9.8   The Broker shall at all times ensure the validity of its liability insurance;

9.9   The Broker undertakes to immediately inform BUZZ of any change, modification and/or update regarding its right to practice and/or its capacity to practice the profession of real estate broker;

9.10  The Broker shall inform the OACIQ and BUZZ if it intends on holding a second job and/or active source of income. Should the Broker intend to do same, it shall complete the second job disclosure form and submit it to the AEO;

9.11  The Broker shall keep itself informed of information issued by the OACIQ, including but not limited to, the OACIQ Bulletin and Info-Compliance;

9.12  The Broker shall have a business continuation plan in place, to be applied in the event the Broker becomes unfit, is deceased, is no longer authorized to practice its activities and/or any other situation causing the Broker to be unable to exercise of its professional functions. Such plan shall be disclosed to BUZZ in writing at the signature of the present Agreement;

9.13  The Broker shall at all times ensure the protection of personal and confidential information;

9.14  The Broker shall at all times maintain up-to-date accounting registers and proper records and shall comply with the record keeping procedure adopted by BUZZ;

9.15  The Broker shall comply with all its continuing education obligations;

9.16  The Broker shall at all times cooperate with BUZZ’s internal programs, including BUZZ’s compliance program;

10.     Advertising, representations and solicitation of clientele

10.1   When advertising, making representations or soliciting customers, the Broker shall, at all times, use its official name, its professional title according to the discipline(s) for which it is duly registered and authorized to practice, and its status as a representative of BUZZ;

10.2   The Broker must identify BUZZ in all professional representations made online or on social media;

10.3   The Broker shall under no circumstances make any representations that are false, erroneous, misleading, or that could be interpreted as such;

10.4   The AEO may at any time, and without justification, order the Broker to remove any publication that does not comply with applicable legislation, that damages or could potentially damage the Broker’s and/or BUZZ’s reputation, or is considered by the AEO to be inappropriate in any way;

10.5   The Broker is explicitly prohibited from advertising any interest rates;

11.  Meeting with clients

11.1   When meeting with a client for the first time, the Broker shall always provide the following information, in writing:

  1. Its full official name;
  2. The professional title and discipline for which it is duly registered and authorized to practice;
  3. Its status as a representative of BUZZ, all while providing BUZZ’s contact details, including the main address, telephone number, email address and official website;

11.2  The Broker shall always inquire about the client’s situation in order to best identify the client’s needs;

11.3  The Broker shall advise clients adequately, solely in matters in which it is authorized to practice;

 

12.   Delegation

12.1   Should the Broker wish to delegate any of its tasks to a third-party, it shall provide BUZZ with a written agreement outlining the tasks being delegated and the duration of said delegation;

 

13.  Vacations and holidays

13.1  The Broker shall be responsible for its own vacations and/or holidays and shall provide an adequate replacement during its absence;

 

14     Resources

14.1  BUZZ undertakes to provide the Broker with all the necessary information, resources and support required for its practice;

 

15.    Miscellaneous provisions

15.1  This Agreement may be executed in several counterparts, each of which when so executed shall be deemed to be an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same agreement;

15.2  The present Agreement annuls and replaces any prior Agreements, either verbal or written. No amendments or modifications to this Agreement shall be valid and binding unless set forth in writing and duly signed by all the parties hereto;

15.3   Where appropriate, the singular shall be deemed to be the plural and the masculine gender either feminine or neuter or vice versa. The capitalized and other subheadings and titles are for convenience or reference only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement;

15.4   This Agreement shall be binding upon the parties hereto, their agents, legal representatives, administrators, successors, transferees and assigns;

15.5    In the event that any one or more of the provisions of this Agreement shall be held to be invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, such provision(s) shall be deemed not to have been written and shall not affect the validity or enforceability of any other provisions of this Agreement;

 

 

15.6   This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with, and governed in all respects by the laws of the Province of Quebec;

15.7   This Agreement has been drawn in the English language at the specific request of the parties hereto. /Cette entente a été redigée en langue anglaise à la demande spécifique des parties.
